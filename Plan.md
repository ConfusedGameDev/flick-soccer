Flick soccer meets balatro 

we will implement a soccer game which is a turn based multiplayer strategy game 

the core idea is as follows 

each player will arm their team with a limited amount of points like in a fantasy soccer 
then the players will create a formation with the 11 players they selected

then players will have to flick like in angry birds pull back aim release to shoot at a dice think mario party.

then the dice will roll and whoever gets the bigger number will start in offensive mode the other player will be in defensive mode

the ball will start at the goalkeeper and the goal is to move it all the way to be close enough to the opponent goal to be able to shoot and try to score

offensive players have 3 flicks per turn while deffensive have 2 flicks.

the players can trade a flick for a dice roll with 2 dices  

matching numbers allow another roll
double 3 or double 6 -> booster pack 
if the summatory is less than 4 then the dices get blocked for 3 turns.

the offensive player can do short and long passes based on how much they pull the player 
or shoot 

the deffensive player can flick the goal keeper to try to stop a shoot or do a tackle in the direction they think the other player will pass.

in the planning stage the players will do their shoots but the ball will not move  until both players are done  once both players are done or the time runs out 60 seconds 
then we see the action.

the ball will follow the attacker path 
if the defender guessed correctly they will overtake the ball and then they will be the attackers  next turn. 

if there is an overtake the defender gets a free dice roll with same rules as before.

when a dramatic thing happens such as 
overtake
goal 
goalkeeper save
corner kick or throw in 
we will play a dramatic animation to make the game feel more alive.
based on tecmos cinematic soccer idea https://www.hardcoregaming101.net/captain-tsubasa/ 
this animations should be static frames with the players moving slowly in 90s  anime style  drawing

we will start with a pool of 26 classic liga mx players from 1960->1987 and 26 modern liga mx players 1988->2007
