Privacy policy

Effective 12 October 2026 · Super Soccer Deluxo (Flick Soccer) by ConfusedGameDev

Short version: the game doesn't collect anything about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the game stores

To remember your progress, the game saves a few small things on your device: your season run, custom kits you paint, whether you have seen the tutorial, and settings such as sound on/off. In the app this lives in the app's own storage; on the web it lives in your browser's local storage for this site. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else, and you can erase it at any time by deleting the app or clearing the site's data in your browser.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no sign-in, does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos, and contains no advertising or analytics code.

Online matches

When you play online, your device connects to our match server to exchange the match itself with your opponent: the 4-letter room code, the kit and squad you chose, your moves each turn and the duel button presses. The server keeps a match in memory only while it is being played and does not store it afterwards. It does not receive your name, device identifiers or anything else about you. Standard connection logs (IP address and time) may be kept briefly by our hosting provider to run the service.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
